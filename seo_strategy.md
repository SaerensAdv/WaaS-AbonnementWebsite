# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages
- Public legal pages
- Public quote-request page (`/offerte`)

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboard (`/app/**`)
- Admin pages (`/admin/**`)
- Authentication and password-reset flows (`/login`, `/signup`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password`)
