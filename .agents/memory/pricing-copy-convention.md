---
name: Pricing copy convention
description: Commercial copy rules for the quarterly subscription model (NL + EN).
---

Across all user-facing copy (home, terms, billing, add-ons, footer, FAQ, SEO meta, nl.ts + en.ts):
- NO free trial ("14 dagen gratis proefperiode").
- NO bare "maandelijks opzegbaar" / "cancel monthly".
- NO "geen contract" / "langdurig contract" → use "geen jaarcontract".
- Prices MAY be shown per month (/maand) as the RATE, but always disclose quarterly billing wherever a price appears.
- Canonical message: "6 maanden minimum · per kwartaal vooruit · geen opstartkosten".

**Why:** the old site mixed "monthly cancellable + trial + no contract" with "6-month minimum + quarterly" — self-contradictory and a legal/trust risk pre-launch.

**Exceptions:** the Maatwerk/custom-project section legitimately keeps opstartkosten (one-off setup fee). A 14-day clause in terms about *material delivery* (art. 5.3) is NOT a trial — leave it.
